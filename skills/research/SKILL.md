# Skill: evidence and market research

**Trigger:** claims require external verification; new algorithms, competitors, platforms, market facts, news, statistics or product claims.
**Output:** claim ledger + dated evidence and caveats.

For each material claim collect: precise wording; type (firsthand/primary/secondary/inference/hypothesis); URL and date; independent corroboration when contested; confidence; whether usable in public.
Prefer official rules, source code, research papers and documented project results. News needs date of event as well as publication date. A source confirms only what it actually says.
Never cite third-party engagement analyses as LinkedIn/X ranking rules; observational correlations are not causal advice. No generic assertion of 'the algorithm likes X' without a first-party technical basis.
Before publication distinguish regulatory proposal from binding rule, product release from roadmap, benchmark validation from official test, and customer signal from actual revenue.
Verify current competitor products for market opportunity posts; identify existing alternatives, feasibility and economic objection. A strong speculative post explicitly says it is a hypothesis.
If unable to fetch material, output `HOLD: unsupported claim` or narrow the post. Record citations as ordinary Markdown links for platform-native reuse.
