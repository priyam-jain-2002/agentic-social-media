# Agentic Social Media

**One verified idea -> zero to three native platform outcomes.** LinkedIn first, X second, technical blog third.

This is the **self-contained installable skill**. All operational references live inside this skill folder so it still works when installed without the rest of its GitHub repository.

## Workflow

1. **Capture:** Take a real project observation or research the past seven days of relevant public AI engineering, developer tools, product and founder developments. Read [research and intake](references/research.md) when finding topics.
2. **Interrogate:** Identify the exact claim, one reader, their problem, why it matters, distinctive angle, evidence and strongest counterargument. Separate observed results from hypotheses.
3. **Reject weak inputs:** A vendor announcement is a signal, not necessarily a post. When original contribution, evidence or relevance is missing, recommend HOLD or SKIP.
4. **Route independently:** Use [platform guide](references/platforms.md). Give LinkedIn, X and blog separate PUBLISH / ADAPT / HOLD / SKIP verdicts; do not mechanically cross-post.
5. **Draft only fitting channels:** Lead with the useful takeaway, then evidence and consequence; write naturally without generic AI slop or manufactured engagement hooks.
6. **Run all gates:** Apply [publishing gates](references/gates.md) covering truth, disclosure, reader value, taxonomy, hashtags, verified mentions, opening, payoff, proof and human voice.
7. **Choose media only if earned:** Prefer actual screenshots, demo clips or accurate data charts. Use [visual and learning guidance](references/visuals.md). No fabricated outcomes or decorative text-heavy graphics.
8. **Output:** Give the reasoning, source URLs and dates, platform decisions, draft(s) only when requested, justified hashtags/people or NONE, proof gaps and next action.
9. **Approval:** Never publish, schedule, engage with social accounts, send messages or modify repositories without explicit user approval. Drafts and research outputs remain in chat or a separate approved private workspace.
10. **Learn:** Compare actual results at matched 24h/72h/7d windows; avoid claiming that one weak post proved an algorithm penalty.

## Non-negotiable standards

- No fabricated first-person experience, claims, numbers, benchmarks, testimonials, affiliations, account handles or source citations.
- Never disclose non-public employer, client or project data without explicit authorization.
- Do not force a post for every channel; a NO POST decision can be correct.
- No AI-cliché intros, faux epiphanies, formulaic suspense, emoji clutter, repetitive one-line paragraphs or generic closing questions.
- No universal promises about ideal hashtag count, length, publishing hour or engagement.
- A skill installation gives instructions, NOT automatic web, GitHub, Notion, LinkedIn, X or blog access.
- Never commit generated drafts, examples of real private posts, analytics or weekly radar results to the public framework repository.

## Output packet

Idea and original insight; one target reader and consequence; evidence and strongest objection; distinct LinkedIn/X/blog verdicts; mandatory gate results; editorial score /40 (heuristic only); justified keywords and optional verified hashtags/mentions; visual choice or NONE; smallest evidence-producing next step.
