# Skill: publishing gates (hard gates first)

**Hard gates — ANY fail = HOLD or SKIP**
H1 Truth: meaningful claims supported or labeled as hypotheses; results not exaggerated.
H2 Disclosure: no sensitive employer/customer/IP/private-person info or misleading affiliation.
H3 Value: a reader learns something nontrivial or gets tangible proof/utility.
H4 Authenticity: not generic AI commentary, synthetic life lesson, fabricated anecdote or engagement manipulation.
H5 Platform fit: identified audience and justified platform selection.
H6 Editorial integrity: no plagiarized content, fake references or misleading graphic.

**Quality rubric** (score 0–5 each, 40 max; scores are editorial heuristics not platform ranking formulas):
Q1 Distinctive original insight / value
Q2 Opening 1–2 lines establish who cares and why
Q3 Payoff delivered early, no delayed reveal
Q4 Evidence / specific firsthand detail
Q5 Natural author voice; no AI-slop
Q6 Identity and reader fit
Q7 Format and visual usefulness on mobile
Q8 Meaningful consequence / takeaway
Default READY: >=34/40 AND Q4,Q5 >=4; otherwise REVISE. Weak idea even with 34 is SKIP. No posting quota.

**Lengths:** LinkedIn test 80–140 words initially (not universal optimum); X one self-contained post by default; blog as long as evidence demands. Trim throat-clearing, never remove vital nuance.

**Mandatory adversarial pass:** What would a skeptical expert object to? What might an employer/customer object to? Does opening overpromise? Is causality inferred? Can one sentence be removed without loss? Would this be worth reading without knowing the author?

**Decision output:** verdict; hard gate status; 8 scores; one most important revision; one sentence of rationale. Don't optimize against unsupported formulae for hashtag count, posting time or image type.
