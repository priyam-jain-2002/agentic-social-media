# Skill: human voice and anti-AI-slop editing

**Voice:** technically exact, curious, grounded, occasionally opinionated, conversational. Write like an engineer explaining a concrete observation to an intelligent colleague. No polished 'thought leader' costume; no inflated authority or faux vulnerability.

## Editing procedure
1. Copy factual claim, author's actual phrasing, audience and emotional temperature from intake. Don't change what happened.
2. Remove all throat-clearing: `Recently I was thinking...`, `In today's rapidly evolving...`, `Excited to share...`.
3. Move the point before the backstory. Replace generalities with concrete nouns, precise verbs, actual choices, consequences or examples.
4. Vary sentence lengths organically. Restore normal paragraphs; one-line fragments only where they genuinely strengthen emphasis.
5. Remove formulaic contrasts and suspense: repeated `not X, but Y`, `here's the catch`, `that got me thinking`, `the real lesson?`, `game-changer`, `unlock`, `revolutionary`, `secret sauce`, `as we navigate`, `this is the future`, `curious what you think?`.
6. Remove emoji/hashtags/exclamation marks unless they serve a specific human use. Never append a generic question.
7. Use real constraints, disagreement, doubt and limitations where relevant. Don't add confessional drama, false modesty or manufactured failure.
8. Compare with source facts. Humanize **style**, never invent details, quotes, claims or unearned certainty.
9. Read aloud test: Would the author naturally say this? If not, rewrite.

**Bad:** 'The next frontier isn't X. It's Y. And that's when it hit me.'
**Better:** 'We could customize the storefront without building order processing. Checkout integration was still our responsibility.'
**Bad:** 'We crushed the competition.'
**Better:** 'We placed 52nd and didn't qualify. The sequence-scoring problem was worth exploring.'

No detector evasion. The purpose is honest, readable writing, not masking generated origin.
