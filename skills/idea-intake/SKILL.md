# Skill: idea intake and analogy finder

**Trigger:** A raw idea, observation, news link, project, or request to turn one analogy into platform content.
**Input:** user statement plus available evidence.
**Output:** canonical idea brief; not a post yet.

1. Distill a single falsifiable core claim: what changed, who cares, why now.
2. Extract factual observation vs inference vs future hypothesis; list proof gaps.
3. Identify the reader's current belief or pain and the new useful belief/action.
4. Find analogies only if their mapping is tight. Record `source domain -> target domain`, exactly what maps, exactly where it breaks. No analogy may substitute for evidence.
5. Identify competing explanation and one expert objection before accepting.
6. Compare novelty with last 5 posts; avoid repeating prior thesis without new proof.
7. Output `ACCEPT / INVESTIGATE / REJECT`. If generic, return a stronger framing or recommend not posting.

**Analogy test:** Can a domain expert describe the mapping accurately and name its limitations? If not, don't use it.
**Ethic:** Never manufacture firsthand experience to fit a topic.
