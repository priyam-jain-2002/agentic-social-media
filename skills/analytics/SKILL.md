# Skill: performance and learning

## Inputs
Platform, post URL, actual copy, publication timestamp, audience/follower baseline, format, impressions/views, likes, replies/comments, reposts/shares, bookmarks/saves (where supplied), link clicks, profile visits and qualified conversations (where available). Mark uncollected fields N/A, not zero.

## Window discipline
Observe at 24h / 72h / 7d. Compare like-for-like windows, similar follower size, content pillar, and format where possible. Segment impressions (distribution) from per-impression engagement (response). Small samples cannot prove an algorithmic penalty.

## Diagnosis
Low impressions + little evidence of exposure: possible audience mismatch/distribution, not proof of bad writing.
High impressions + low qualified engagement: reader promise, specificity, audience or mismatch needs testing.
High likes but irrelevant commenters: reach may not advance desired profile.
Measure meaningful replies, qualified inbound, demo visits and partnerships as appropriate.

## Experiments
Change one major dimension at a time (hook, artifact, format or reader) across multiple posts. Log hypothesis beforehand, result and alternative explanations afterward. Don't infer 'algorithm triggers', deterministic best time or universal format rules.

Historical snapshots are private, provisional and dated. Never put someone's profile analytics into public content without approval.

## Output
`Observed; comparison baseline; hypothesis; confidence; next test; stop-doing` in a brief review. Always separate facts from causal speculation.
