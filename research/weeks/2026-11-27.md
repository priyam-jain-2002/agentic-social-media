## Deep dive 1 — Durable workflow retries
**Source fact:** Vercel reports (in vendor benchmarks) a new Workflow SDK 5.8 engine with lower orchestration overhead; the announcement explicitly says speculative execution requires idempotent steps or disabling retries where side effects cannot safely repeat. Numbers vary by benchmark and are not generalized to all workloads.

**Reader:** engineers building long-running agents, automation or order processing. **Question:** How do you make a retryable task safe without duplicate external side effects?
**Original angle:** A short open-source demonstration showing safe idempotency keys for a payment-like operation vs a no-idempotency email side effect.
**Counterargument:** The performance improvements are Vercel's own benchmarks; correct retry behavior is independent of Vercel and cannot be reduced to one SDK setting.
**Proof needed:** runnable mock side-effect test, not actual customer orders. If it isn't built, frame as cited technical commentary, not firsthand results.
